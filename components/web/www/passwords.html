Seed phrase

12 words (BIP39) in the store key with the PIN, kept on this device only: the store files alone never open. Every device of one store needs the same phrase.

 

Passwords and TOTP secrets never leave the device: a box left empty keeps the one it has, a new value replaces it. Codes are for the current 30 s window. Every change is written to vault.bin at once. The order here is the device's: ↑ ↓ move a record by one place, ⇈ ⇊ to the top / bottom; a new one goes last.
